The 5GB rule

Is Snapchat deleting Memories? What is actually true

Updated November 2026 · about 5 min read

If you have ever saved a snap to Memories, this affects you. Snapchat capped free Memories storage, said it would delete what was over the limit, and then changed its mind. There is a lot of old information still going around. This is the plain-English version of where things stand and what to do.

What changed

For years, Snapchat Memories were effectively unlimited and free. In September 2025, Snapchat introduced a 5GB free storage cap. Anything beyond 5GB needs a paid plan. Snapchat promised affected users 12 months of temporary storage before anything changed.

At first, Snapchat's help page said recent snaps over the limit would be deleted. That sentence is gone. The page now answers the question directly: “Snapchat will not automatically delete your Memories because you do not upgrade.”

What happens in January 2027

Instead of deleting, Snapchat will archive. Starting in January 2027, Memories that are more than one year old and are not part of your oldest 5GB are archived. They stay stored on Snapchat and show up as thumbnails, but you need a paid plan to open, edit or share them. What stays open for free:

  • Your oldest 5GB of Memories.
  • Anything you saved in the last year.

Everything in between is what gets archived. If you have used Snapchat for years, that middle stretch is most of your library. Snapchat says no Memories will be archived before January 2027, and that it will give notice in the app before anything changes for you.

The short version: nothing is being deleted. But if you have more than 5GB of Memories and do nothing, from January 2027 you will be looking at thumbnails of your older photos and videos unless you pay. Exporting them is free and takes a few minutes of your attention.

Your three options

1. Pay Snapchat every month

Snapchat sells storage plans. In the US the 100GB Memories plan is $1.99 a month or $20 a year, and Snapchat+ and Snapchat+ Platinum include 250GB and 5TB. Prices vary by region. This keeps your Memories open, but they stay in Snapchat's cloud and you keep paying to look at your own photos.

2. Do nothing and live with thumbnails

Your older Memories stay on Snapchat as thumbnails. You can still see that they exist, but you cannot open, edit or share them without upgrading.

3. Export everything and keep it yourself

You can request a full copy of your Memories from Snapchat and save it to your own computer. Snapchat lists downloading as one of your two choices for archived Memories. It is free, and it means you never pay a subscription to look at your own photos. The catch is that Snapchat's export is awkward, which is the next thing to understand.

The catch with exporting

When you download your Memories from Snapchat, the files arrive stripped of their dates and locations. Every photo and video looks like it was taken today, with no GPS, and any captions or stickers come as separate files. Import that into Google Photos or Apple Photos and years of memories pile up on a single date with no map and no order.
